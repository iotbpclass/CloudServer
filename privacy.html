自动工程实作课 · 隐私权政策

本网站（https://iotbpclass.github.io/CloudServer/）是自动工程实作课的上课管理工具，只给本课程的老师和学生使用。

我们收集什么

学生姓名、班级、年级、学校、学生 PIN、上课出席与完成进度、老师写的学习总结；家长姓名和电话（只有老师看得到，用来联络家长）。

资料放在哪里、给谁看

资料存放在 Supabase（新加坡机房）。学生只看得到自己的进度和老师发给他们班的文件；家长资料、PIN 和老师备注只有老师看得到。我们不会把资料卖给或分享给任何第三方。

Google Drive

老师可以把本网站连接到课程自己的 Google Drive，用来自动备份上课资料。本网站只使用 drive.file 权限：只能读写它自己建立的备份文件，看不到 Drive 里的其他文件。老师可以随时在网站的「设定」或 Google 账号的「第三方存取权」里断开。

删除资料

家长或学生如果要删除资料，请联络老师，老师会在网站里删除；备份会在 60 天内自动清掉。

Privacy Policy (English)

This site is a class management tool used only by the teacher and students of the Automation Workshop course. It stores student names, classes, grades, schools, student PINs, attendance, progress and teacher summaries, plus parents' names and phone numbers (visible to the teacher only). Data is stored on Supabase (Singapore). Students can see only their own progress and their class's files. We do not sell or share data with third parties.

The teacher may connect the course's own Google Drive for automatic backups. The site uses only the drive.file scope, so it can access only the backup files it creates, and can be disconnected at any time. To delete data, contact the teacher; backups are removed automatically within 60 days.
